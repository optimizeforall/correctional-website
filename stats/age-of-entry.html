Published figure

12.8

Average age of entry among domestic minor sex-trafficking survivors who reported being forced into pornography production — younger than street (13.8) or online prostitution and stripping (14.2).

What this number is

Among survivors in this study who reported forced pornography production, the average age they entered that form of exploitation was 12.8 — younger than other venues in the same sample. Age of entry is not age of first internet use; it is when they were forced into that market.

Caveats

n=260 domestic minor sex-trafficking survivors. Averages hide range. This is not the average age of everyone in the commercial sex industry, and it is not a claim about consensual adult production.

What the 90% is for

This is why children come first in the 90% allocation. Forced pornography production of minors is CSAM. The work is identification, removal from the situation, and years of care — not a one-night rescue statistic.

01

The Pledge

A brother pledges for the 140 days — $50 a month or $250 once. Recommended $150 a month, or $750 once. 90% to the fight. 10% runs Correctional.

WHAT YOU GIVE

Same mission either way. Five monthly charges, or one payment that covers the 140.

  • Monthly: minimum $50, recommended $150 — five charges, then it stops
  • One payment: minimum $250, recommended $750 — the same five months, paid once
  • 90% goes to documented anti-trafficking work. 10% runs Correctional. You can send that 10% to the fight too.
  • You set the amount on the signup page — after you take the covenant
  • Every dollar is tracked and published — see the ledger →
WHERE IT GOES

Your money becomes rescue work you can count.

At the recommended $150 a month, 90% — $135 — is roughly three days of aftercare housing for a survivor, or one border interception, or four investigation hours, every month of the mission. These are partner-average unit costs, not promises.

02

The Covenant

Before your first weekly call, you purge what feeds the sin and commit to the fight.

THE PURGE

Destroy or delete everything that keeps you bound — before your first weekly call.

  • Delete saved images, videos, and stashes on every device
  • Cancel subscriptions — OnlyFans, premium sites, apps
  • Unfollow accounts, creators, and triggers on social media
  • Remove photos of past relationships that pull you back
  • Turn on accountability software if you use it
THE COMMITMENT

140 days. Four men. Honest weekly calls. Goals counted forward, not backward.

  • Track days in prayer and days in the Word — not days since a slip
  • Show up to your cohort call every week, prepared and on time
  • Speak honestly about victory and failure; what is said stays in the room
  • Answer when a brother calls — day or night, you pick up
  • Keep your pledge — $50 a month or $250 once; recommended $150 / $750
03

Your Cohort

You are placed with three other men for a 140-day mission. Same start date. Same standard.

4 Men per cohort
140 Day mission
1 Private Discord channel

Cohorts start when four vetted men are ready — not on a rolling open door. You will know your cohort mates by name before the first call. If someone drops out, the group decides together whether to continue or pause.

04

The Mission

140 days. 20 weekly calls. Formation, not attendance — you train to become a soldier in Christ's army.

MISSION OBJECTIVES

What you build over 140 days — with your cohort holding the standard.

  • Design your morning and evening prayer regimen — and keep it
  • Understand spiritual warfare and your position as a soldier in Christ's army (Ephesians 6)
  • Identify your triggers and eliminate them, one by one
  • Become regimented — whatever you specifically need to do to become a true soldier
STANDING ORDERS

Rules of engagement. They hold at all times, from day one to day 140.

  • When an urge hits — call a brother. Before, not after.
  • If you go through with it — call a brother. Same order. No hiding either way.
  • Confession happens out loud on the weekly cohort call — not in a text, not in your head
THE WEEKLY CALL

One hour. Same day each week, for 20 weeks. Prayer, Scripture, honest accounting.

  • Open in prayer — name what you are fighting and what you are building
  • Report days in the Word and days in prayer since last week
  • Confess slips without performance or competition
  • Set one concrete aim for the coming week
  • Close with prayer

DAY 140 — THE DEBRIEF You give your testimony to your cohort — what was broken, what got built — and you choose what's next: serve another tour, or help lead a new cohort of four.

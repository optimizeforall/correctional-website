Pornography and the market

Published figures on how pornography shows up in prostitution, trafficking, and demand. Each card is one study or intake report.

49%

Of 854 people currently or recently in prostitution across nine countries said pornography was made of them while they were in prostitution. 47% were upset by attempts to make them imitate acts from pornography.
